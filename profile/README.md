# CTRL e.V. – Construction Technology and Robotics Laboratory

Student-led association at RWTH Aachen. We build, test and explore robots for construction through events, workshops and hands-on projects.

**Focus**

- **Construction tech:** digital fabrication and smart materials
- **Automated robotics:** kinematics, ROS and machine precision
- **Applied workshops:** deep-dives into modern tooling
- **Hardware projects:** building physical prototypes
